How a centre and its healers stay in step

Both desks share one secure database and one Google sign-in, so nothing has to be copied across or sent on WhatsApp.

  1. At the centre

    A Subject arrives

    They fill in the centre's intake form, or the centre adds them by hand. They wait in the Unallocated list.

  2. At the centre

    The centre head allocates

    Two taps assign the Subject to a healer, along with a healing plan.

  3. On the healer's desk

    The Subject appears

    They show up on the healer's desk straight away, next to the healer's own Subjects.

  4. Back to the centre

    Each healing is logged

    The healer records scans, healings and notes. The centre's Today view updates as they do.

Questions

Who is Healer's Desk for?

Energy healers who run their own practice, and healing centres where a centre head works with a team of healers. Many healers use both.

What does it cost?

A healer's desk is free to start. If you run a centre, sign in and set it up, or write to us at healersdesk@gmail.com and we'll help you get going.

Can I belong to a centre and keep my own practice private?

Yes. Subjects a centre allocates to you appear on your desk alongside your own. The centre sees only the Subjects it allocates, never your personal ones.

Can a healer work with more than one centre?

Yes. A healer can join several centres with the same Google account they use on app.healersdesk.com.

Do I need a password?

No. Everyone signs in with their Google account.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Both desks work in the browser on a phone, tablet or computer, and a healer can add My Healing Desk to their Home Screen so it opens like an app.

Does energy healing replace medical care?

No. Energy healing complements medical care; it does not replace it. Healer's Desk is a record-keeping tool for healers, not a medical record.
