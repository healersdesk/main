Your clients' trust, protected

People share health details and personal struggles with you. Healer's Desk is built so that information stays between you and them.

  • Only you see your desk. Every record is locked to your Google account at the database level; no other healer can see it.
  • Photos stay private. Client photos sit in private storage and open only through short-lived links inside your desk.
  • Reports show only what's safe. Shared links never include medical history, private notes, phone numbers or fees.
  • Your data is yours. Download everything as a file, or delete your desk and all its records yourself, any time.
