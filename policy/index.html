Our policies

These policies explain how Healer's Desk (www.healersdesk.com) handles information, the rules for using the app, and how payments, cancellations and refunds work.

Effective from 1 October 2026.

In short: each healer's desk is private to their own Google account. We use the information on it only to run Healer's Desk. We do not sell it, show ads, or look at client records except when you ask us for help or the law requires it.

Healer's Desk is free to use today. If paid plans are introduced, you will be told first, and the refund terms below will apply.

Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026 · Updated to include Google Calendar

1. Who we are

Healer's Desk ("Healer's Desk", "we", "us") is a web app that helps energy healers manage client intake forms, energy scans, healing sessions, payment records and progress reports. You can reach us at healersdesk@gmail.com or +91-950-983-1551.

This policy is written to meet the requirements of India's Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, as they apply.

2. Healers and their clients

Two kinds of people have information on Healer's Desk:

  • Healers, who sign in with Google and run a desk.
  • Clients, whose details a healer adds, or who fill in a healer's intake form or send feedback from a progress report.

Each healer decides what client information to collect and why. For client information, the healer is responsible for it (the "data fiduciary" under Indian law) and Healer's Desk stores and processes it on the healer's behalf. If you are a client, your healer is your first point of contact for questions about your information; you may also contact us and we will help.

3. What we collect

About healers:

  • From Google sign-in: your name, email address and profile picture. We never receive your Google password.
  • What you enter: your name, practice name, WhatsApp number, city and chosen intake link.
  • Technical information our hosting providers log automatically, such as IP address, browser type and the time of requests, used for security and to keep the service running.
  • If you choose to connect Google Calendar: the email address of the Google account you connect, and a permission token from Google (stored encrypted) that lets Healer's Desk manage the one calendar it creates for you.

About clients (entered by the healer or submitted through the healer's intake form):

  • Contact and personal details: name, WhatsApp number, email, date of birth, gender, occupation and city.
  • Wellbeing information: concerns, symptoms and their intensity, goals, and medical history or conditions the client chooses to share. This is sensitive personal information and is collected only with the client's consent on the intake form or as recorded by the healer.
  • An optional photograph, used by the healer for distance healing.
  • Session records: energy scans and observations, healing dates, healer's notes and comments, case fees and payment records, and feedback the client sends.

4. How we use information

  • To provide Healer's Desk: show each healer their own desk, deliver intake forms to the right healer, and display progress reports that a healer chooses to share.
  • To sign healers in securely and keep accounts safe.
  • To answer support requests and send important service notices (for example, a change to these policies).
  • To comply with the law and respond to valid legal requests.

We do not sell personal information, rent it, use client records for advertising, or contact a healer's clients for our own purposes. Healer's Desk staff do not view client records, except when a healer asks us for help with a specific problem, or when required to investigate abuse or comply with the law.

5. Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. If a healer connects it, Healer's Desk asks Google for permission to create a separate calendar called "Healer's Desk" in that healer's Google account, and to add, change and delete events in that calendar only. Healer's Desk cannot see, read or change any other calendar or event in the healer's Google account.

For each healing session, the event contains the client's name, the date and time, any session notes the healer wrote, and a link back to My Healing Desk. Events are created, updated or deleted when the healer schedules, edits, completes, cancels or deletes a session. The healer can change the session length and reminder, and can disconnect at any time from Desk settings (and choose to remove the "Healer's Desk" calendar). Disconnecting revokes our access with Google. You can also remove access from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy. Healer's Desk's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google data only to show and keep the healer's own sessions up to date in their calendar. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, use it to train AI models, or let people read it except with the healer's permission for support, for security, or where the law requires.

6. Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to run Healer's Desk. They process information only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase, for the database, sign-in and private photo storage.
  • Vercel, for hosting the website.
  • Google, for "Sign in with Google" and, if you connect it, Google Calendar.
  • jsDelivr and Google Fonts, which deliver the app's code libraries and fonts to your browser and may see your IP address when they do.

These providers may store or process data on servers outside India. We choose providers that protect data with industry-standard security. We may also disclose information if required by law, court order or a government authority acting lawfully, or to protect the safety of any person.

A shared progress report link shows only the client's first name, healer and practice name, scan results, number of healings and any comments the healer chooses to share. It never shows medical history, private notes, phone numbers or fees. The healer can switch the link off at any time.

7. Security and storage

  • All traffic to Healer's Desk is encrypted using HTTPS.
  • Each healer's records are protected by database-level access rules, so a healer can only read and change records on their own desk.
  • Client photos are kept in private storage and are opened only through short-lived links inside the healer's desk.
  • Sign-in is handled by Google; we do not store passwords.

No online service can be perfectly secure. Healers should keep their Google account protected (for example with two-step verification) and sign out on shared devices. If we become aware of a breach affecting personal data, we will inform affected healers and the authorities as the law requires.

8. How long we keep information

We keep a healer's desk and its records for as long as the account is active. A healer can delete individual client records at any time, and can delete their whole desk from Desk settings → Delete my desk, which removes the desk, every client record, every photo and the Healer's Desk login. Deleted information is removed from our live systems immediately and may remain in encrypted backups for a limited period (normally up to 30 days) before it is overwritten. We may keep limited information longer where the law requires it.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you can ask to access, correct, update or erase your personal information, withdraw consent you have given, and raise a grievance. You may also nominate another person to exercise these rights if you die or become unable to do so.

  • Healers can correct details in Desk settings, download all their data as a file, or delete their desk themselves.
  • Clients should first ask their healer, who can correct or delete their record. If you can't reach your healer, write to us and we will help.

Withdrawing consent does not affect what was done before you withdrew it. We will reply to requests within 30 days.

10. Children

Healers must be at least 18 years old to create a desk. A healer may keep records for a client under 18 only with the verifiable consent of that child's parent or lawful guardian, which the healer is responsible for obtaining.

11. Cookies and browser storage

Healer's Desk uses your browser's storage to keep you signed in and to remember small preferences (such as whether amounts are hidden). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service or the law changes. We will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, tell healers inside the app or by email before the change takes effect.

13. Grievance Officer and contact

If you have a question, request or complaint about your personal information, please contact our Grievance Officer. We will acknowledge your message within 48 hours and aim to resolve it within 30 days.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective 1 October 2026

1. Current pricing

Healer's Desk is free to use at present. No payment is taken to create or use a desk, so there is nothing to refund today. If you have been charged by anyone claiming to be Healer's Desk, please contact us straight away.

2. Cancelling a paid plan

If paid plans are introduced:

  • Plans will be billed in advance for each billing period (for example, monthly or yearly).
  • You can cancel at any time from Desk settings or by emailing healersdesk@gmail.com from the email address on your desk.
  • After you cancel, your plan will not renew. You keep paid features until the end of the period you have already paid for, and your desk and data remain available on the free plan.

3. When refunds apply

You will receive a full refund for a paid plan if:

  • you ask within 7 days of your first-ever payment for a plan;
  • you were charged twice for the same period, or charged an incorrect amount;
  • you were charged for a renewal after you had cancelled; or
  • a fault on our side made the paid features unavailable for more than 72 hours in a row during your billing period (in this case we may refund the affected portion instead).

Apart from these cases, payments are not refundable for partly used billing periods, unused time after cancellation, or a change of mind after the first 7 days.

4. How to request a refund

  • Email healersdesk@gmail.com from the email address on your desk, with the payment date, amount and transaction or order ID.
  • We will acknowledge your request within 2 working days and tell you whether it is approved.
  • Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 working days. Your bank or payment provider may take a further 5–10 working days to show it in your account.

5. Payments for healing sessions

Healer's Desk does not collect or process payments between healers and their clients. The "Energy exchange" section is a private record that healers keep of what clients have paid them. Fees, cancellations and refunds for healing sessions are agreed directly between the client and their healer, and Healer's Desk is not responsible for them. Clients with a question about a session payment should contact their healer.

6. Closing your desk

You can delete your desk at any time from Desk settings → Delete my desk. This permanently removes your desk, client records and photos. Please download your data first if you want to keep a copy.
